# Line J — "The Cursed Disciple" (Xiao Ye `{J}`; opens at Origin Spirit, grade ≥ 5)

Dialogue of the line (approved 2026-11-19, coded: `ModMain/Story/Lines/LineJ_*.cs`, texts in `story_localText.json`). Design: `../06-Storylines-Draft.md` section 4; conventions:
`00-Conventions.md`.

Voice of `{J}`: silent at first, then fierce; mirrors how he is raised (`mercy`, `strength`, `freedom` counters).
`{S3}` is the sect that marked him; `{SM}` its sect master.

## J0. The execution ground (grade 5+, in a town; 0.5 % a month)
1. *A crowd in the square. A boy of thirteen kneels on the stones, a black mark crawling up his neck.*
   - **Elder of {S3}:** "A demon seed. Better now than when he is grown."
2. *The boy looks at no one. Then at you.*
   - ▸ Stop the execution (battle with the elders) ▸ Buy him (spirit stones, the elder sneers) ▸ Watch → line closed
3. **{J}:** "…" *He follows you without a word.* (a child record until 16)

## J1. First lessons (5 years; 3 moments)
1. **The bird.** *He breaks a wounded bird's neck "so it stops hurting".* ▸ Teach mercy ▸ Teach strength ▸ Let him choose
2. **The bully.** *A sect boy throws stones at him.* ▸ "Forgive him." ▸ "Hit back harder." ▸ "Walk away; you owe no one."
3. **The question.** **{J}:** "Am I a demon?" ▸ "You're whatever you decide." ▸ "You're mine. That's enough." ▸ "Yes. Use it."
- Missed moments → he runs away (J3 comes earlier).

## J2. The mark wakes (time limit 1 year)
1. *A message from {T}: "Your boy is burning the market."* *Black fire. {J} in the middle of it, eyes white.*
2. ▸ A suppression pill ▸ Fight him to exhaustion (battle with `{J}`) ▸ Let it burn out (the town is damaged)
3. **{J}** (after): "I heard voices. They knew my name. They knew yours."

## J3. The hunters (time limit 2 years)
1. *Elders of {S3} at your gate.* "Hand him over. The mark has woken; we know."
2. → Battle with the elders.
3. **The truth.** *A dying elder laughs.* "You fool. The mark is {SM}'s own sin — he cut it out of himself and put it in a child."

## J4. The heart of the boy
1. *{J}'s breakthrough. The demon speaks through his mouth:* "Take my power, boy. Your master will die one day. I won't."
2. *The lessons decide* (counters): mercy → he refuses; strength → he takes it and masters it; freedom → he asks you.
3. ▸ (asked) "Refuse it." ▸ "Take it. You're stronger than it." ▸ "It's your choice."

## J5. The student surpasses
1. ▸ Side by side against `{SM}` (battle) ▸ A final duel with `{J}` (real battle)
2. **{J}:** "You never asked what I wanted to be. …I wanted to be like you. Annoying, isn't it?"
3. → Ending.

## Endings J
- **Righteous Disciple**: joins your team for free; a preferred heir option.
- **Demon Disciple**: he leaves for the demonic path; a recurring enemy, boss of a town war. *"Thank you, master. For everything."*
- **Sealed**: you seal the mark and his cultivation; he becomes a mortal — and, if line H runs, a cook at the Roadside Inn.
