# Line I — "The Sealed Tomb" (Elder Gu `{Q}`; opens at Golden Core, grade ≥ 4)

Dialogue of the line (approved 2026-11-19, coded: `ModMain/Story/Lines/LineI_*.cs`, texts in `story_localText.json`). Design: `../06-Storylines-Draft.md` section 4; conventions:
`00-Conventions.md`.

Voice of `{Q}`: polite, scholarly, quotes old texts; cold underneath. `{O2}` is the tomb owner (an ancient
cultivator, a voice).

## I0. A map in a corpse (grade 4+, outside a town; 0.5 % a month)
1. *A dead cultivator under a pine, a torn half-map in his fist.*
2. *A voice behind you.* **{Q}:** "Ah. You found my colleague. He owed me the other half of that." *He holds up his own half.*
   - **{Q}:** "Partners, then? The tomb of the Azure Sage, sealed for nine thousand years. Plenty for two."
3. ▸ "Partners." ▸ "I'll go alone." (rival: he races you, I2 harder) ▸ Take his half by force (battle)

## I1. The team (time limit 1 year)
1. **{Q}:** "The outer halls need four. Bring two you trust — or two you can afford to lose."
2. ▸ Bring two team members (hired companions count) ▸ Hire two from the town ▸ Go with Gu alone
3. *Night before the tomb. {Q} reads by the fire and never sleeps.* ▸ Ask about his sect ▸ Watch him

## I2. The outer halls (time limit 2 years)
1. **The puppets.** *Bronze guardians wake as the seal cracks.* → Battle.
2. **The three doors** (choices): "Water flows down; the sage looked up." ▸ Left ▸ Middle ▸ Right (one right; wrong: a trap, HP loss)
3. **The poisoned treasure.** *A pile of spirit stones glitters in a side room.* ▸ Take it (poison: a month of weakness) ▸ Leave it
- **Late → "Looted Tomb"**: another sect opened it first; only the outer treasure is left.

## I3. The knife in the back
1. *At the inner gate, {Q} smiles for the first time.* "The seal needs a sacrifice. One of you. I did say 'afford to lose'."
2. → Battle with Gu and his hidden disciples (your team fights with you).
3. **After.**
   - Won: *Gu's jade slip lists six earlier 'partners'.* ▸ Spare him (his sect hostile, he returns later) ▸ End him
   - Lost: *You wake outside the tomb, robbed. Gu is inside.* → I4 against him later.

## I4. The tomb owner
1. *A coffin of green jade. A tired voice from inside.* **{O2}:** "Nine thousand years. Who knocks?"
2. **{O2}:** "I sealed myself to escape the heavens' notice. Take my legacy and I fade at last. Free me and I walk again. Or seal me back, and the world forgets me."
3. ▸ Take the legacy ▸ Free him ▸ Seal him again → ending.

## Endings I
- **Heir of the Tomb**: +5 to your best weapon basis; Gu's sect hostile. *"Use it better than I did."*
- **Freed Soul**: a yearly counsel (a pill or a rare herb of your grade). *He wanders the world, a curious old man.*
- **Sealer**: the righteous sects' gratitude (contribution and reputation).
- **Looted Tomb**: only the outer treasure; Gu's bounty on you.
