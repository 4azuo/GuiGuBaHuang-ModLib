Voice of `{K}`: warm, practical, scolds `{p}` like family ("You again? Sit. Eat."). `{K2}` restless and bright;
`{K3}` calm, with his grandmother's laugh. The inn is in the player's first town `{T}`.

## H0. A leaking roof (first 5 years; player enters the inn of `{T}`; 1 % each visit)
1. *Rain drums through the roof of a half-built inn. A young widow is catching it in cooking pots.*
   - **{K}:** "We're not open. …Oh, sit anyway, you're dripping on my floor."
2. **The loan shark.** *A fat man with two thugs kicks the door open.* "Payment's due, Mei Niang."
   - ▸ Pay her debt (spirit stones; intimacy +150) ▸ Chase them off (battle) ▸ Finish your soup and leave
3. **The first bowl.**
   - **{K}:** "I'll call it the Roadside Inn. Grand name, small inn. Your bowl is free — forever. Don't argue."
   - → The inn opens (a resting place: mood, news of the month).
- **Left** → she opens it anyway, smaller (no free bowl).

## H1. The wedding (10 years)
1. *A red paper on the inn door: "Closed for a wedding — friends welcome."*
2. **{K}:** "He guards caravans. Smells of horses. Laughs too loud. I like him." ▸ A gift (any item) ▸ A toast ▸ Both
3. *She pours the first cup for you, before the groom.* "For the one who fixed my roof."

## H2. The fire (25 years; time limit 2 years)
1. *The inn is a black shell. {K} sits on the steps with a sleeping boy in her arms.*
   - **{K}:** "Bandits. They wanted the strongbox. My husband… he was in the way."
2. ▸ Hunt the bandits (battle) ▸ Pay to rebuild (spirit stones) ▸ Sit with her a while, then go
3. **After.**
   - Hunted / paid: *A new roof, a new sign. The boy runs between the tables.* **{K}:** "His name is {K2}. Say hello to your uncle."
   - **Late / nothing** → the inn is gone: "Ashes" (she moves away; one letter a decade).

## H3. The son who wants to fly (40 years)
1. *{K2}, now sixteen, blocks your way at the door.* **{K2}:** "Teach me. I've read every cultivation book in this town. Twice."
2. **{K}** (from the kitchen): "Absolutely not. Cultivators die. Cultivators leave."
   - ▸ Take him as your disciple ▸ Send him to a sect ▸ Talk him out of it (he runs the inn)
3. **{K}:** "…If he goes, you bring him home for New Year. Every year. Promise."

## H4. Mei Niang grows old (60 years, or when her life ≤ 5 years)
1. *Her hands shake when she pours now. She still refuses help.* **{K}:** "Sixty years and you still look twenty. Rude."
2. **The last meal.** *She cooks everything you ever ordered, all at once.*
   - **{K}:** "Keep coming. Even when I'm not here. Especially then."
3. *She dies in her sleep in the room above the kitchen.* → Taoist log; a grave behind the inn.

## H5. The hundredth year (100 years)
1. *A banner: "The Roadside Inn — 100 years." {K3}, her grandchild, greets you by name before you speak.*
   - **{K3}:** "Grandma said you'd come. She said: 'the one who never ages, never pays.'"
2. **The feast.** *Everyone still alive from your long life sits at the long table* (allies of the other lines' good endings).
3. ▸ A speech ▸ A toast to Mei Niang ▸ Pay for everyone, at last
   - → Ending.

## Endings H
- **Inn of Ages** (the inn stands at H5): resting there restores mood and HP; a yearly meal; news of the world.
- **Disciple Lineage** (`{K2}` became your disciple): he is a preferred heir option, like A Li (line E).
