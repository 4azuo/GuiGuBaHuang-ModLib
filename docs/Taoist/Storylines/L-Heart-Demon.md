# Line L — "The Heart Demon" (`{Z}`; opens at Enlightenment, grade ≥ 8; the last line)

Dialogue of the line (approved 2026-11-19, coded: `ModMain/Story/Lines/LineL_*.cs`, texts in `story_localText.json`). Design: `../06-Storylines-Draft.md` section 4; conventions:
`00-Conventions.md`.

Voice of `{Z}`: the player's own words, twisted; patient, intimate, never shouts. The dreams and ghosts are read
from the story log (endings of A-K): a missing line is skipped, so the line works with any history.

## L0. Dreams (grade 8+, the first seclusion / time skip of 5+ years at that realm, or 1 % a month)
1. *You dream of a tea house that no longer exists.* (one ending per night, e.g.)
   - Yue Han (A, Companion): *"You never told me why you helped me. I stopped asking."*
   - Old Huang (B, any): *"Still counting coins, eh? Count the years, cultivator."*
   - Lan Qiu (G): *"Rival. Are you still climbing? Who for?"*
   - Mei Niang (H): *"Sit. Eat. You look thin, even in a dream."*
2. *Each morning the dream is clearer, and one face in it is your own.*
3. **{Z}** (in the last dream): "We should talk. You've been avoiding me for a few centuries."

## L1. The mirror lake
1. *A lake so still it shows the stars at noon. Your reflection steps out of it.*
2. **{Z}:** "Three choices. The three you think about at night." (read from the log: a handed-over Yue Han, a refused Lan Qiu, a burned inn...)
3. For each: ▸ "I was wrong." ▸ "I'd do it again." ▸ "It doesn't matter now."

## L2. The ghosts
1. *The lake fills with shapes: the enemies your choices made* (Traitor Yue Han, Demon Disciple Xiao Ye, Lady Honglian if Kneel...).
2. → Battle against their shadows.
3. **{Z}:** "They are not real. You are fighting yourself either way."

## L3. The copy
1. *{Z} draws your sword. Your stance, your technique, your breath.*
2. → Battle: a copy of you (same stats, same arts).
3. Won → L4. Lost → *You wake in your cave, a year gone, a realm's worth of exp lost.* (retry after 1 year)

## L4. Clear heart
1. **{Z}** (kneeling, or laughing): "So. What am I to you?"
2. ▸ "A part of me. Stay quiet." (accept) ▸ "Nothing. Go." (deny) ▸ "Everything I need." (embrace)
3. *The lake goes still. The stars go back to the night.* → Ending; an epilogue line for every finished storyline in the log.

## Endings L
- **Clear Heart** (accept): a permanent breakthrough bonus; the log epilogue of every line.
- **Scarred Heart** (deny): the demon returns every 20 years (L3 again).
- **Embraced** (embrace): alignment turns demonic; great power, the righteous world hostile.
