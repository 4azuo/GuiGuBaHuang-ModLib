# Line K — "The Thunder Remnant" (`{U}`; opens at Soul Formation, grade ≥ 7)

Dialogue of the line (approved 2026-11-19, coded: `ModMain/Story/Lines/LineK_*.cs`, texts in `story_localText.json`). Design: `../06-Storylines-Draft.md` section 4; conventions:
`00-Conventions.md`.

Voice of `{U}`: ancient, weary, half-mad, grandiose; speaks in the thunder (narration over a background, no NPC).
`{V2}` is his dao companion of 30,000 years ago (a name in the ruins).

## K0. A voice in the thunder (grade 7+, a breakthrough or a lightning trial; once)
1. *The ninth bolt hangs in the sky and does not fall. Something looks down through it.*
   - **{U}:** "Another one. Another little candle climbing toward the storm."
2. *The bolt falls — softer than it should.* **{U}:** "Live. I am curious."
3. *A scar of lightning stays on your arm. It hums before storms.* → Story item "Thunder Scar".

## K1. The broken platform (time limit 3 years)
1. *The scar pulls toward the far north.* (a map point)
2. *A stone platform split in two, blackened. Lightning puppets wake.* → Battle.
3. *Words cut into the stone:* "I climbed alone." **{U}:** "I did. Remember that, little candle."

## K2. His descendants (time limit 2 years)
1. *A sect in white robes kneels when it sees your scar.* "The Ancestor's mark! Give it to us — or give yourself."
2. ▸ Fight them (battle) ▸ Bargain (spirit stones and a promise) ▸ Show them the platform's words
3. **{U}:** "Children of my children. They pray to me. I never answered. I could not."

## K3. The truth
1. *In the ruins, a second name under his: {V2}.*
2. **{U}:** "She held the ladder while I climbed. I kicked it away so the heavens would see only me. They saw. They struck. I deserved it."
3. **{U}:** "Give me your body for one tribulation and I will climb again — properly. Or only stand with me while I try. Or send me to sleep."

## K4. The second tribulation
1. *The sky over the platform turns white.* (lightning trial battle, `RealTrialEvent`)
2. ▸ Let him guide you (he lends his power) ▸ Fight his remnant inside the storm ▸ Refuse him
3. **{U}** (last words, whatever the choice): "Tell her— no. There is no one left to tell."

## Endings K
- **Guided**: the next breakthrough is easier (tribulation damage -30 %, once).
- **Devourer Defeated**: his remnant destroyed; a lightning technique bonus and thunder resistance.
- **Second Chance**: he is reborn as an NPC, a powerful friend — or, by a hidden choice (lying to him in K3), an enemy.
